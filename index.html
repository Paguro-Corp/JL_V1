Three campaigns

One clutch, one night, one woman.

Each campaign runs for one week: a hero static that works on its own as an ad, then a five-slide carousel that tells the night around it. The three nights are different on purpose, so the grid never looks like one photo shoot cut into pieces.

Campaign 01 / Elina PLUS Malbec

Vintage

 Campaign 02 / Elina PLUS Mirrored Black Chrome

Intermission

 Campaign 03 / Finley Touring Red

Facebook

  • AudienceReaches clients who spend more time on Facebook than on Instagram, often an older, established audience.

  • Carousel adsEach carousel can run as a Meta carousel ad, one slide per card, linking to the clutch page.

Vintage

Campaign 01 / Elina PLUS / Malbec, gold hardware

The dinner is over and she is leaving on her own, in a car she chose to keep. Everything in the frame has age: the stone, the roadster, the oak. The only thing that shines is the clutch.

Light

Publishes onInstagramFacebookFeed and Stories, cross-posted the same day

Why it works

  • It links the clutch to the brand's automotive roots without a logo or a showroom: a car she drives, at night, by herself.
  • Malbec reads as the color of the evening itself, wine, stone and lantern light.
  • The pose is a moment between two actions, so nothing is staged for the product.

Intermission

Campaign 02 / Elina PLUS / Mirrored Black Chrome, chrome hardware and chain

Fifteen minutes between two acts. The room is moving, talking, crossing in front of her. She does not need to move to be the center of it, and the mirror on the clutch carries the whole night on its surface.

Light

Publishes onInstagramFacebookFeed and Stories, cross-posted the same day

Why it works

  • Composure is the brand's word for her. Stillness inside a moving crowd shows it without a caption.
  • The mirror finish turns every scene into part of the product, so the clutch tells the story by itself.
  • A mixed crowd, mostly in black with emerald and navy, keeps the event real.

Between Places

Campaign 03 / Finley / Touring Red, chrome hardware, no chain

The night also happens on the street, between one plan and the next. She leaves an opening and walks to a dinner in a black coat and dark denim. The clutch is the one evening piece she never takes off.

Why it works

  • It brings back the street direction of the first proposal and moves it to the night, where the brand belongs.
  • Touring Red is car paint. Against asphalt, concrete and passing tail lights it is the only color in the frame.
  • Luxury streetwear here means wool, denim and leather pumps, never sportswear or logos.

Product fidelity

The scene changes. The clutch does not.

Every image is generated from the official packshots and checked against them before it is shown. These are the details that are never reinterpreted.

Silhouette

Elina PLUS is a domed horizontal oval. Finley is a slim rectangle with rounded corners and an almost flat face.

21.6 × 13.3 × 5.7 cm / 21 × 10.8 × 3.6 cm

Frame

A thin continuous metal frame runs along the seam between the two machined shells, in the color of the hardware.

Gold or chrome

Clasp

A round knob at the exact top center. A thin ring rests flat on the frame, seen almost edge-on, and a solid oval tab drops onto the front.

Same clasp on every rigid clutch

Chain

Intermission shows the original wheat-braided chain, clipped at a single ear on the left end of the frame. Vintage and Between Places show the clutch without it.

One anchor, left side only

Color

Official names only, matched to the photographs: Malbec, Mirrored Black Chrome, Touring Red.

Lacquer sealed in ceramic coat

The women

Real skin, real age between late 20s and 40, natural hair, contemporary styling. Different women, different nights. Each campaign keeps its own protagonist; guests are all different people, in many colors, not only black.

No grey hair, no retouched skin
