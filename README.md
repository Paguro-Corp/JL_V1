## 1. Executive Summary & Delivery Overview

This master delivery package represents the culmination of the **Jeffrey Levinson v3** campaign overhaul. Moving decisively away from generic CGI and 3D mockups, the project establishes a cohesive, museum-grade nocturnal editorial identity for Jeffrey Levinson's aerospace monocoque clutches ($1,395 – $3,200 USD).

### Key Accomplishments:
1. **24 Final Approved Photographic Works (2K Resolution, Strict 4:5 Aspect Ratio):**
   - **Campaign 01 (Vintage):** 8 images of *Elina PLUS* in deep Malbec ceramic lacquer with 18K gold-plated hardware. Completely chainless.
   - **Campaign 02 (Intermission):** 8 images of *Elina PLUS* in Mirrored Black Chrome (gunmetal liquid mirror) with gunmetal latch and a single spiga wheat chain anchored strictly to the left side. Right side unpierced and polished monolithic.
   - **Campaign 03 (Between Places):** 8 images of *Finley* in Italian competition Touring Red ceramic lacquer with polished chrome hardware. Completely chainless.
2. **Elimination of Visual & Ergonomic Presentation Flaws:**
   - **Problem A Resolved:** The cramped 228px 5-column carousel has been replaced with the **Interactive Editorial Showcase** featuring a generous 560px 4:5 main display stage, dynamic metadata panels, and a 5-thumbnail filmstrip navigator.
   - **Problem B Resolved:** Alternates `-07` and `-08` are curated into an **Editorial Diptych Spread ("Frames from the Night")** with high-end magazine styling and channel contextualization (*VIP Lookbook* & *Press Spread*).
   - **Problem C Resolved:** An executive **Commercial Intent & Value Card** has been added to every campaign, linking the imagery directly to buyer psychographics, price architecture, and engineering justification.
3. **Master Strategic Documentation (4 Comprehensive Volumes):**
   - `docs/CREATIVE_AUDIT_V3.md`: Critical diagnosis and 7 design pillars of luxury.
   - `docs/CAMPAIGN_STRATEGY_AND_PUBLICATION_RATIONALE.md`: Comprehensive strategy and 24-piece publication matrix with 10 mandatory fields per image.
   - `docs/HTML_REDESIGN_SPEC.md`: Technical design system, component architecture, and responsive breakpoints.
   - `docs/ANTIGRAVITY_FINAL_EXECUTION.md`: Front-end implementation, QA protocols, and Git deployment guide.

## 3. How to Open and Review the Presentation

### Local Viewing:
Double-click `index.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari, or Mozilla Firefox).
- The presentation is 100% self-contained with relative image paths.
- No local web server or Node.js environment is required for local viewing.
- Google Fonts (`Bodoni Moda`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `IBM Plex Mono`) load automatically when connected to the internet; fallback system serif and sans-serif fonts are built in for offline environments.

### Interactive Features to Test:
1. **Hero Triptych:** Hover over any of the three hero images for optical zoom and focus; click any card to smoothly scroll to that campaign's chapter.
2. **Interactive Showcase:**
   - Click any thumbnail in the 5-frame filmstrip below the stage to instantly view the frame at 560px with updated piece codes, lens specs, lighting setup, and commercial narrative.
   - Use the `← Previous` and `Next →` buttons or the left/right arrow keys on your keyboard.
3. **Editorial Diptychs:** Observe the side-by-side archival curation of pieces `-07` and `-08` with VIP Lookbook and Press Spread tags.
4. **Commercial Intent Cards:** Review the buyer psychographics, price architecture, and engineering value equation at the foot of each chapter.
5. **Responsive Fluidity:** Resize your browser window from 1440px to 1024px, 768px, and 390px (mobile) to verify that no clutch is cropped or distorted.

2. Under **Build and deployment > Source**, select `Deploy from a branch`.
3. Choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
